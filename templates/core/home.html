Grassroots Impact

Activities Conducted by Meezan Welfare and Charitable Trust

We are dedicated to serving all needy individuals and communities with compassion, unity, and social responsibility. Below are the key initiatives we carry out:

1
Communal Harmony
Iftar Koota Program

A special interfaith gathering during Ramadan inviting people from different religions to promote unity, mutual respect, and brotherhood in Harapanahalli.

Ramadan Annual Support
2
Hunger Relief
Free Ration Kit Distribution

Providing essential monthly ration supplies (rice, flour, dal, cooking oil) to widows, orphans, disabled individuals, and struggling families in need.

Monthly Drive Support
3
Youth Empowerment
Spoken English Classes

Helping local students and youth enhance their English communication, grammar, and interview skills to unlock better higher education and job opportunities.

Ongoing Classes Support
4
Emergency Healthcare
Blood Donation Camp

Voluntary blood donation drives mobilizing youth to support medical emergencies, trauma care, and save lives in regional hospitals.

Periodic Camps Support
5
Preventive Health
Health & Screening Camp

Offering free general medical check-ups, blood pressure & glucose tests, free medicines, and raising vital awareness about community health and hygiene.

Free Medical Checkup Support
6
Civic Sanitation
City Cleaning Campaigns

Organizing waste disposal and cleanliness drives across public streets of Harapanahalli, especially during Eid festivals and community gatherings.

Eid Sanitation Support
7
15 Major Circles
Free Drinking Water

Supplying clean, chilled, and safe drinking water across 15 major circles of Harapanahalli during hot summers for commuters, laborers, and pedestrians.

Summer Relief Kiosks Support
8
Primary Care Center
Meezan Clinic & Pharma

Affordable primary healthcare facility and subsidized dispensary for the Harapanahalli community, ensuring no family suffers from lack of medicines.

Permanent Facility Support
9
City-Wide Treasury
Baitul Maal for Harapanahalli

Creating a structured, audited financial support system to collect Zakat & Sadaqah and assist poorest families with dignity, micro-grants, and 100% transparency.

Zakat & Micro-Aid Support
